**What I was working on.** Turning the channel-structure thinking into specific
video ideas rather than more categories.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** After two weeks of trying to settle what
*types* of video the channel should hold, I expected to need the taxonomy before
I could propose anything concrete. I had it backwards.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **The abstract question would not close, so I stopped asking it.** Weeks 17
  and 18 both ended with "still Phase 1." The blocker I named last week was that
  choosing content types means deciding who the channel is for — and I could not
  decide that in the abstract. Proposing actual videos turned out to be a way
  around it: a specific idea carries its audience with it, so the suggestions
  imply an answer I could not reason my way to directly.
- **"A few" is the honest count and it is also the weakness.** This is a first
  set, not coverage. I do not know whether these are the right ones, or
  representative, or enough to pattern-match a channel shape from.
- **I did not send them.** Same week the intro/outro went out and came back
  confirmed, these stayed with me. I have no good reason for that; the branding
  just felt finished and these did not.
- **I wrote nothing down — again.** This is the part worth being blunt about.
  My Week 18 entry said in plain words that the next session needed a written
  deliverable, "even a wrong one." I then produced the deliverable and still did
  not commit it. Three weeks, 23 hours on this thread, and the repository holds
  descriptions of work rather than the work.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand that a concrete proposal can resolve an abstract question that
direct reasoning could not. Two weeks of "who is this channel for" went nowhere;
one week of "here are some videos" produced an implicit answer. Working forward
from instances beat working down from categories.

I do not know whether the suggestions are any good, because nobody has read
them — including, in any durable form, me. And I now notice a pattern across
three weeks: on this thread I consistently finish the thinking and skip the
recording. The branding thread does not have this problem, and the difference is
that branding produces a file by default while planning only produces a file if
I decide to make one.
